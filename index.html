How Planetary Data Helps Us Understand Space

Planetary science relies heavily on observational data collected by probes, rovers, and orbital telescopes. By examining orbital mechanics, surface temperatures, atmospheric pressure, and mass density, scientists can reconstruct the origins of our solar system and predict long-term celestial evolution.
